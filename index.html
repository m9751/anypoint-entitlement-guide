How to Check Your Anypoint Platform Entitlements

Your MuleSoft subscription includes a set of licensed resources — integration flows, vCores, gateways, and more. This guide shows you exactly where to find that information in the Anypoint Platform.

ℹ️
Two things to check, two different places.
Flow usage lives in Usage Reports (username icon → Usage Reports).
vCore capacity lives in Access Management (gear icon → Access Management → Subscription → Runtime Manager).
Both are covered below.

1 Check Your Flow Usage (Mule Runtime)

Flows are the active integrations running inside your Mule applications. Your contract sets a limit on how many can run simultaneously — this is where you see where you stand.

How to get there

  1. Log in to Anypoint Platform at anypoint.mulesoft.com
  2. Click your username icon in the top-right corner
  3. Select Usage Reports from the dropdown
  4. Use the Product dropdown to select Mule Runtime
  5. Choose Monthly Usage Report for a summary, or Daily Usage Report for trends
Username icon › Usage Reports › Product: Mule Runtime

What you will see

Flow countActive flows running across your org
By Business GroupUsage per team or division
By ApplicationWhich apps consume the most
Prod vs. SandboxFilter by environment type
Monthly peakHighest simultaneous flow count
Export as CSVDownload for offline analysis
💡

Good to know: Only flows that actively trigger integrations count against your limit. Internal helper flows used purely for code organization do not count.

⚠️

Access requirement: Usage Reports are only visible at the root organization level with the Usage Viewer permission. If you do not see the option, contact your org administrator.

2 Check Your vCore Usage (Compute Capacity)

vCores are the computing resources your Mule applications run on in CloudHub. Your contract gives you a set amount to allocate across environments. This page shows your purchased limit versus what is currently in use.

How to get there

  1. Click the gear icon (settings) in the top-right navigation bar
  2. Select Access Management
  3. In the left-hand navigation, scroll to the Subscription section
  4. Click Runtime Manager
  5. Use the Business Group dropdown to switch between groups if your org is divided
Gear icon › Access Management › Subscription › Runtime Manager

What you will see

Each resource shows a progress bar and an "X / Y in use" count — your current usage against your purchased limit. The page also shows your Subscription Tier and Expiration Date.

Production vCoresFor production environments
Sandbox vCoresFor sandbox / test environments
Design vCoresFor design environments
VPCsVirtual Private Clouds / Private Spaces
Network ConnectionsVPNs or Transit Gateways
Flex GatewaysSmall and large managed gateways
✅

Flows and vCores are two separate limits. You can be within your vCore capacity but approaching your flow limit, or vice versa. Check both independently.

3 Set Up Alerts

Anypoint Platform automatically notifies you when your monthly entitlement consumption hits key thresholds. Make sure the right person on your team is set up to receive them.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

API instances count until deleted — not just deactivated

If you manage APIs through Anypoint API Manager, those instances count against your API entitlement until they are explicitly deleted. Simply undeploying or deactivating an application does not remove it from the count.

The last 3 days of data may still be finalizing

Usage data refreshes twice a day. The most recent three days are still being calculated. For an accurate end-of-month snapshot, wait until the period has fully closed before exporting.

Usage Reports are not your invoice

These reports show consumption for visibility and planning purposes. Your actual billing is managed separately through your Salesforce contract.

If you are on Usage-Based Pricing (UBP)

Consumption is tracked and reconciled differently. Your Customer Success Manager is the best person to review your UBP position.

Government Cloud and Private Cloud Edition

Usage Reports are not available in these environments. Contact your account team for an entitlement summary.
